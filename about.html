About Our Foundation

The Sarah Mini Humanitarian Foundation is a non-profit organization established to serve marginalized populations worldwide. Guided by an Islamic values framework of community equity, sustainability, and absolute preservation of human dignity, we create structural intervention frameworks to challenge poverty loops.

Our Strategy

We do not believe in short-term fixes. True social advancement requires a holistic methodology that addresses immediate human vulnerabilities while handing over foundational tools—such as long-term micro-grants and full educational access—to guarantee generational independence.
