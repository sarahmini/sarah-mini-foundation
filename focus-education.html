Educational Assistance & Scholarships

Education is the most powerful tool for systemic transformation. We provide bright, dedicated young minds facing poverty with everything they need to enter full-time academia.

Core Programs:

  • Sarah Mini Scholars Fund: Multi-year institutional scholarships covering tuition, registration, and laboratory fees.
  • Academic Mentorship Program: Linking sponsored students with industry professionals for career guidance.
  • Supply & Tech Grants: Providing necessary hardware, laptops, and instructional textbooks to students.
